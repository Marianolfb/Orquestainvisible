About us

La Orquesta Invisible is a contemporary tango orchestra from Buenos Aires led by bandoneonist Julio Coviello, an artist with a career spanning more than 25 years in the Argentine tango scene.

The ensemble, formed by musicians from different generations and backgrounds, reinvents the traditional orquesta típica format through original compositions, collective creation, and a powerful acoustic sound that is rooted in Tango's Golden Age but driven by a modern and urban energy.

In its debut year, the project was selected for the official programming of the Buenos Aires Tango Festival, thanks to Coviello's trajectory in fundamental groups of the genre such as Cuarteto Cedrón and Orquesta Típica Fernández Fierro.

With a strong stage presence, its own repertoire, and a vision of tango as a living cultural heritage, La Orquesta Invisible offers an authentic and avant-garde Argentine sound for world music festivals and international stages.
