About the orchestra

Orquesta Invisible is a contemporary “orquesta típica” directed by bandoneonist and composer Julio Coviello. It distinguishes itself by making collective composition the central axis of its artistic identity, a rare practice within the genre. Founded in Buenos Aires in 2025 through an open call, the orchestra brings together musicians from different generations and backgrounds, from performers trained in traditional orchestras to figures from the contemporary and academic scenes, creating a space where diverse paths merge into a common language. Based on the classic orquesta típica format, the project proposes a modern reinterpretation of tango that combines the depth of tradition with a living creative process, in which the music is built collaboratively and in constant transformation.

Artistic project

Orquesta Invisible develops a working method based on improvisation, the exchange of ideas and their subsequent development into a score, generating a repertoire that integrates different authorships within a shared sonic identity.

This approach introduces a dynamic that is uncommon in tango, historically centered on the figure of the individual composer. On stage, the orchestra delivers an intense and dynamic performance that combines original works by its director, collective compositions and new co-written pieces, including the voice as part of the group’s sonic universe.

The result is an evolving musical language that keeps its anchor in the tradition of the orquesta típica while projecting itself toward a contemporary practice of tango.

Members

Julio CovielloDirector & bandoneon+

Julio Coviello is a bandoneonist, composer and cultural manager with more than 25 years of career in tango. He has been a member of fundamental groups of the genre such as Cuarteto Cedrón, Orquesta Típica Fernández Fierro and Tango Cañón.

He is also the founder of La Tierra Invisible, an independent cultural space devoted to live music, where the orchestra holds its regular concert series and its creative laboratory. He maintains an active teaching role as a bandoneon professor at four of the main conservatories in Buenos Aires, contributing to the training of new generations of performers. His career brings together performance, composition, teaching and artistic production, with a comprehensive view of contemporary tango.

Rodrigo AlmonacidBandoneon+

Rodrigo Almonacid is a distinguished Argentine bandoneonist and composer from Comodoro Rivadavia, Chubut. He played in tango ensembles in his hometown. In Buenos Aires he has been part of La Hoguera del Tango, the Orquesta Típica El Afronte and the Orquesta Típica Di Pasquale, and he is currently a member of the Orquesta Invisible.

Mariano BurnengoBandoneon+

Mariano Burnengo is an Argentine musician and bandoneonist trained in the classic and contemporary tango scene of Buenos Aires. His artistic outlook is enriched by his background as a multi-instrumentalist, playing violin and guitar.

He began his formal studies at the Escuela de Música Popular de Avellaneda (EMPA) and developed his technique and style under leading figures of the genre, studying bandoneon with Ramiro Boero, Eva Wolf, Chino Molina and Santiago Arias. He also took the specialization seminar on the work of Astor Piazzolla at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes (UNA), taught by Matías Gobbo, completed two years of orchestral practice and arranging at the Escuela Orlando Goñi and privately with Julián Peralta, and trained at the Academia Tango Club with Rodolfo Roballos and Quena Taborda.

As a member and co-founder of independent projects, he played in the student orchestra Desborde, and in early 2025 he joined the Orquesta Invisible.

Paula CorrarelloViolin+

Paula Corrarello is an Argentine violinist specializing in tango and popular music. Her training and career are tied to prestigious teaching institutions and tango orchestras in Buenos Aires.

She has been a member of the Orquesta Escuela de Tango «Emilio Balcarce». She is currently studying in the Orchestral Academy program at the Instituto Superior de Arte del Teatro Colón and completing her degree in Music (violin) at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes.

Santiago MooreViolin+

Santiago Moore is an Argentine violinist working in both classical music productions and modern tango.

He is a core member of the Orquesta Aeropuertos Argentina, conducted by Néstor Tedesco, and of the Orquesta Sinfónica Juvenil Nacional «Libertador San Martín». In musical theatre, he was the solo violinist in the successful Disney+ production Cuando Frank conoció a Carlitos.

He trained at the ISATC and continues his studies at the UNA (violin). His teachers include Bruno Giuntini, Oleg Pishenin, Roberto Calomarde, Sergio Polizzi and, currently, Sebastián Prusak.

Mabel EtchevarríaViolin+

Mabel Etchevarría is an Argentine violinist devoted to tango, orchestral and chamber music. Her career stands out for her active involvement in independent ensembles and in projects preserving musical heritage.

  • She is a member of the Orquesta Sinfónica Juvenil Nacional «Libertador San Martín», with which she toured Japan in 2024.
  • She has worked with the commission honoring composer Alfredo Schiuma at the General San Martín Conservatory of Music, performing operatic works adapted for youth orchestras and chamber ensembles.
  • She has recorded on albums such as «Nuevas Caretas» by the group Dottopa, bringing the violin to contemporary productions.
  • She is a founding and permanent member of the Orquesta Invisible.

She trained at the Conservatorio Schiuma in General San Martín.

Fernando EstrupViola+

Fernando Estrup is an Argentine violist, a graduate of the Conservatorio Superior de Música Manuel de Falla, where he now teaches. His career stands out for its versatility, from classical music to tango.

  • He has played in ensembles such as the Orquesta Académica del Teatro Colón, the UNLa Chamber Orchestra and the Florencio Varela Municipal Orchestra, among others.
  • He teaches in the institutional orchestra of the Conservatorio Manuel de Falla and plays in the orchestra of the conducting program at the UNA.
  • In tango, he was part of the Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce and is a member of the Orquesta Típica Ciudad Baigón, with which he has recorded several albums and toured internationally. He is a founding member of the Orquesta Invisible.
Dolores VelascoCello+

Dolores Velasco is a cellist working across classical music, popular music and tango. She performs in chamber concerts, orchestral projects and private events in Argentina and Belgium. She is a member of the Orquesta Típica Invisible, devoted to contemporary and new tango.

She studied at the Universidad Nacional de Lanús (UNLa), then earned her Bachelor's degree at the Koninklijk Conservatorium in Brussels and her Master's at the Royal Conservatory of Mons in 2023. She has played with the Orquesta Académica del Teatro Colón, the Orquesta Libertador General San Martín and the Ushuaia International Festival Orchestra, performing in venues across Argentina and Europe.

She took part in the Latin American Cello Festival and the Santa Catarina International Music Festival (FEMUSC, Brazil). She is also active in chamber and popular music, teaches cello and performs regularly in concerts and live events.

Patricia SzilagyiPiano+

Patricia Szilagyi has been a tango pianist since 2009. She works as a performer in tango shows, as a composer and as an arranger. From 2017 to 2025 she was musical director and arranger of the tango orchestra La Rantifusa with singer Natalia Martínez (FNA Creation Grant 2021, and Hugo del Carril Award for best composition, with a released album). She plays in the Orquesta Invisible, contemporary and new tango, and in TIPAS, a trio of traditional milonguero tango, on a European tour in 2026.

She was part of the Orquesta Escuela Emilio Balcarce under maestro Víctor Lavallén in 2024/2025. Since 2013 she has been an accompanist for classical, contemporary, folk and tango dance at the ISA Teatro Colón, and since 2018 she has created soundscapes for stage works at the Universidad Nacional de las Artes. She has also played with groups such as Quinteto Negro La Boca, with concerts and international tours between 2009 and 2014.

Alex ValdésDouble bass+

Alex Gallardo Valdés is a Chilean double bassist and tango musician. He holds a degree in music (electric bass) from the Universidad de Valparaíso, Chile, is a music teacher graduated from the Universidad Andrés Bello, Chile, and is completing a Master's in Education Policy and Management at the Universidad Nacional de Luján, Argentina. He specializes in arts education.

He started playing music at 15 in the city of Quilpué and has since played many styles as an electric bassist in the Valparaíso region. He developed his tango side playing double bass with the Quinteto Tanguedia of Valparaíso from 2016 to 2019. He has lived in Argentina since 2019, playing with several tango ensembles. To date he has recorded 4 tango albums: 2 with Chilean groups and 2 with Argentine groups.

Between 2022 and 2024 he was the double bassist at Madero Tango Iguazú, also touring Brazil and Uruguay in 2023. He currently plays double bass with several ensembles in Buenos Aires, such as Sexteto Alba, Yeta Tango and Té Negro, his main project being the Orquesta Invisible.

Sofía VernaVocals+

Sofía Verna (Buenos Aires, 2004) is a singer and pianist from the popular music scene, actively involved in tango and folk projects. She has performed in many formats, from her own projects to collaborations with various ensembles, building a broad musical and interpretive language.

She trained as a performer specializing in piano at the ESEAM Juan Pedro Esnaola and went on to study contemporary music, deepening her knowledge of jazz and vocal improvisation.

Among her most notable projects is Virado, a Latin American music group with which she represented Argentina at the World Bolero Festival in Mexico, with a sound that combines roots and the present and strongly reaches new audiences.

Her artistic path is marked by a constant expressive search and a flexible approach to repertoire. Sofía belongs to a new generation of musicians bringing fresh, authentic value to popular music.

Repertoire and album “Huella”

The orchestra’s first album, Huella (2026), synthesizes its artistic identity and its way of working. The repertoire brings together compositions by Julio Coviello, written over more than two decades, along with works created collectively by the group, among them new tangos and a candombe.

It was presented live on May 30, 2026 at Palacio Libertad, Buenos Aires. Available on Spotify and all platforms.

“A footprint (huella) is a sign of movement and is built by many people walking along the same path,” says the director.
