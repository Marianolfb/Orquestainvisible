Music auditions, talks, and empanadas

Buenos Aires, 2026 — By Alex Valdés

It all started on February 18, 2025, when a call for musicians reached my social media: Julio was inviting people to join the Orquesta Invisible, which would begin to take shape in March. At that moment, I was finishing a substitute teaching position as a music teacher at a high school, sitting in the teachers' lounge.

Rehearsals were scheduled for Wednesdays from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM, a time when I was usually teaching. However, fate seemed to arrange things: the regular teacher announced her return, and suddenly, that time slot became free for me. I spoke with Julio, and although we had to postpone the audition because it coincided with my last day of substituting, I was finally able to attend the following week.

I arrived on a Wednesday in March with my double bass at La Tierra Invisible. Julio welcomed me as the host, and several musicians were already present. While I was setting up my instrument by the piano, the dear Patricia appeared, with whom I had shared music some time ago in Madero Tango - Iguazú, in the north of Argentina in the province of Misiones. Her presence filled me with joy and gave me extra confidence for the audition.

We played the pieces that had been sent to us: El Cedrón y la Lechuza and Lengua Seca. From the very first moment, I was struck by the sonority and energy arising from that music. At the end, Julio gathered us all to tell us about the proposal: to play monthly at La Tierra Invisible, work on his repertoire, and also compose new pieces during the year. The idea was also to record that entire process on an album towards the end of the year.

To close the day, we shared some empanadas, and Julio told us that he would contact each of us in the following days to confirm who would be part of the project. Three or four days later, I received his message: I had made it into the Orchestra. The joy was immense. Not only because of the music, which I loved, but also because of the human atmosphere that had been generated from the very first meeting.

So it was that, the following Wednesday, the selected musicians met for the first rehearsal of the new Orquesta Invisible. We played the audition pieces again, but this time they sounded even more solid, more alive. Then we began to explore new pieces from the repertoire.

The joy was immense. Not only because of the music, which I loved, but also because of the human atmosphere that had been generated...

As the weeks went by, Wednesdays turned into a ritual: a space for encounter, creation, and community. Making music, supporting each other, sharing a mate that circulated from hand to hand, along with chipás and cookies, while conversations flowed between multiple topics.

That's how the rehearsals went, as well as the performances at our home, La Tierra Invisible, that place that became a refuge and made us feel deeply sheltered. There, three original compositions were also born: Centenera, followed by Clave Blanca and Ceniza, which finished consolidating the repertoire.

During the year, we also had the opportunity to perform at the Buenos Aires Tango Festival, at the Adan Buenosayres Cultural Center. And as the grand finale of 2025, in December we entered Doctor F studio to record our first album. They were two intense and beautiful days, where the music of the Orquesta Invisible of that year was recorded.

Today I write these words while listening to the result of that entire process: our first album, HUELLA, has been released. From a distance, because I returned to Iguazú for a few months. But thinking about the return for the official presentation in May, when we celebrate our first year.

I am deeply grateful to my orchestra colleagues —Patricia, Dolores, Fernando, Mabel, Santiago, Paula, Mariano, and Rodrigo— and, especially, to Julio, for this journey and for the happiness of sharing this very significant project together.

This is only a part of the invisible story that we continue to write day by day.
