Mabel Etchevarría is an Argentine violinist devoted to tango, orchestral and chamber music. Her career stands out for her active involvement in independent ensembles and in projects preserving musical heritage.

  • She is a member of the Orquesta Sinfónica Juvenil Nacional «Libertador San Martín», with which she toured Japan in 2024.
  • She has worked with the commission honoring composer Alfredo Schiuma at the General San Martín Conservatory of Music, performing operatic works adapted for youth orchestras and chamber ensembles.
  • She has recorded on albums such as «Nuevas Caretas» by the group Dottopa, bringing the violin to contemporary productions.
  • She is a founding and permanent member of the Orquesta Invisible.

She trained at the Conservatorio Schiuma in General San Martín.
