Dolores Velasco is a cellist working across classical music, popular music and tango. She performs in chamber concerts, orchestral projects and private events in Argentina and Belgium. She is a member of the Orquesta Típica Invisible, devoted to contemporary and new tango.

She studied at the Universidad Nacional de Lanús (UNLa), then earned her Bachelor's degree at the Koninklijk Conservatorium in Brussels and her Master's at the Royal Conservatory of Mons in 2023. She has played with the Orquesta Académica del Teatro Colón, the Orquesta Libertador General San Martín and the Ushuaia International Festival Orchestra, performing in venues across Argentina and Europe.

She took part in the Latin American Cello Festival and the Santa Catarina International Music Festival (FEMUSC, Brazil). She is also active in chamber and popular music, teaches cello and performs regularly in concerts and live events.
