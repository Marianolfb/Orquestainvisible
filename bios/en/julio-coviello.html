Julio Coviello is a bandoneonist, musician, educator and composer, renowned for his solid instrumental training and his way of performing. At just 18 he became a key member of the Orquesta Típica Fernández Fierro, which renewed the contemporary tango scene. There he played, composed and arranged tangos until 2016, while also forming his own group. Between 2018 and 2022 he joined the legendary Cuarteto Cedrón.

He also founded the Cuarteto Coviello (2009-2014), and since 2014 he has led Tango Cañón with Nicolás di Lorenzo. With this duo he has released three albums, and together with DJ Tandas Nuevas they co-host a milonga every Friday.

Coviello is also the founder and manager of La Tierra Invisible (Av. Del Barco Centenera 1099, Buenos Aires), a lovely venue in Parque Chacabuco devoted to popular music.

He has also played with the Quinteto Agustín Guerrero, Derrotas Cadenas and Altertango, among others, and formed a duo with guitarist Edgardo González. He is deeply committed to teaching and holds the bandoneon chairs at four music conservatories in the Buenos Aires area: Manuel de Falla (Buenos Aires City), Julián Aguirre (Banfield), Alberto Ginastera (Morón) and Alfredo Schiuma (San Martín).
