Fernando Estrup is an Argentine violist, a graduate of the Conservatorio Superior de Música Manuel de Falla, where he now teaches. His career stands out for its versatility, from classical music to tango.

  • He has played in ensembles such as the Orquesta Académica del Teatro Colón, the UNLa Chamber Orchestra and the Florencio Varela Municipal Orchestra, among others.
  • He teaches in the institutional orchestra of the Conservatorio Manuel de Falla and plays in the orchestra of the conducting program at the UNA.
  • In tango, he was part of the Orquesta Escuela de Tango Emilio Balcarce and is a member of the Orquesta Típica Ciudad Baigón, with which he has recorded several albums and toured internationally. He is a founding member of the Orquesta Invisible.
